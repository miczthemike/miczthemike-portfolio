Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how Spin the Wheel (the "App"), created by MicztheMike, handles your information.

Install Spin the Wheel on Google Play

1. Data Collection & Privacy

CRITICAL PRIVACY GUARANTEE: All data you input into this app, including prize names, settings, and custom logos, is stored ONLY on your local device. We do not have servers, and we never upload, transmit, or share your data over the internet.

2. Custom Logos and Images

The App allows you to customize the wheel by selecting an image/logo from your device's gallery.

  • Local Storage Only: When you select a logo, the App saves a reference to that file or a copy within the App's private internal storage on your phone.
  • No Uploading: Your images are NEVER uploaded to the web, cloud, or any external server. They remain strictly within your own app data on your phone.

3. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions for specific functions:

  • Vibration: Used solely to provide haptic feedback while the wheel is spinning.
  • Media/Storage: Used solely to allow you to select a custom logo for your wheel and to share screenshots of your wins.

4. Screenshot Sharing

If you choose to use the "Share" feature to share a winning result, the App generates a temporary image file on your device. This image is only shared with the external applications you explicitly choose (e.g., messaging apps or social media). We do not collect or store these shared images.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
