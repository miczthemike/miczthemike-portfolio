About Cliniqua

Quality is at the heart of the name.

Cliniqua's official website says the center was established in March 2024 by Dr. Zenaida G. Dizon, a neurologist from Dagupan City, to serve the healthcare needs of Alaminos.

The “Qua” in Cliniqua stands for quality. The center's stated promise is to provide quality, affordable, and timely results, with a vision of convenient, connected care—from consultation and laboratory testing to imaging and patient management.
